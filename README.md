## 🛏️ Product Specifications

### 1. Vacuum-Compressed Mattresses (真空压缩床垫系列)
- **Core Materials:** High-density support foam, gel-infused memory foam, and individually wrapped pocket springs.
- **Compression Technology:** Roll-packed in heavy-duty waterproof vacuum bags and shipped in compact export cartons to maximize logistics efficiency and reduce shipping volume by up to 70%.
- **Certifications:** CertiPUR-US, OEKO-TEX Standard 100, and ISO9001 quality management standards.
- **Customization Options:** Thickness (ranging from 6 to 14 inches), firmness levels (Plush, Medium, Firm), and customized private-label branding.

### 2. Modular Floor Sofas (模块化组合沙发系列)
- **Frame Structure:** Kiln-dried solid wood and high-resilience web suspension.
- **Modular Design:** Freely combinable sectional layouts tailored for modern living spaces, apartments, and hospitality projects.
- **Fabrics & Upholstery:** Premium stain-resistant tech fabrics, breathable linen blends, and pet-friendly velvet options.
- **Packaging:** Knock-down space-saving packaging designed for safe e-commerce transit and easy home assembly.
